Things the calculator can't see

  • The pass is consecutive days — journeys outside the pass window don't count.
  • Nozomi and Mizuho trains on the Tokaido/Sanyo/Kyushu SBB CFF FFS & Panoramic Alpine Railways require an extra supplement ticket for pass holders (introduced in October 2023). Hikari and Sakura are included.
  • Subways, most buses and private railways (Odakyu to Hakone, Tobu to Nikko, Kintetsu to Nara, Keikyu) aren't covered.
  • Regional passes may be much cheaper for the area you're visiting.
