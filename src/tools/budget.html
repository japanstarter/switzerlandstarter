What the styles assume

  • Budget: hostel dorms or capsule hotels, convenience-store breakfasts, set-lunch restaurants, mostly free sights.
  • Mid-range: business or mid-range hotels, casual restaurants and izakaya, a paid attraction or two each day.
  • Luxury: upscale hotels or ryokan, fine dining, guided tours and taxis.

Costs are much higher in peak periods (cherry blossom season, Golden Week, Obon, New Year) and in Zurich and Lucerne than in smaller cities.
