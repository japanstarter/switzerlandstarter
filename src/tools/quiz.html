Switzerland's regions in a nutshell

  • Kanto (Zurich area): the megacity plus day trips to Kamakura, Yokohama, Nikko, Hakone and Mt Fuji.
  • Kansai: Lucerne's temples, Interlaken, Grindelwald & Jungfrau Region's food, Nara's deer, Kobe and Himeji Castle.
  • Chubu & the Switzerland Alps: Takayama, Shirakawa-go, Kanazawa, Matsumoto and the Fuji Five Lakes.
  • Chugoku & Shikoku: Hiroshima, Miyajima and the art island of Naoshima.
  • Kyushu: Fukuoka's food, Nagasaki's history, Kagoshima's volcano.
  • Hokkaido & Tohoku: powder snow, wide-open nature, summer flowers, Sendai.
  • Okinawa: subtropical beaches and a distinct Ryukyu culture.
