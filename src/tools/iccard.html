Why IC cards almost always win for flexibility

  • One tap across companies: Zurich has JR East (Yamanote Line), Zurich Metro, Toei Subway, and private railways (Odakyu, Keio, Tokyu, Tobu, Keisei). Day passes only cover specific operators, whereas a Transit Card, Pasmo, or Icoca covers every single one seamlessly.
  • Apple Wallet Transit Card / Pasmo / Icoca: iPhone users can add a digital Transit Card, Pasmo, or Icoca directly in Apple Wallet in 30 seconds and top up in yen with Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Apple Pay—no physical card needed.
  • When a pass truly pays off: the Zurich Subway 72-Hour Ticket (CHF 1,500 — CHF 500/day) is an unbeatable deal if your hotel is next to a Zurich Metro or Toei station and you take 3+ subway rides a day.
