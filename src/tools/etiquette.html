Three golden rules in Switzerland

  • No tipping anywhere: restaurants, taxis, hotels, bars, hairdressers, and tour guides do not use tipping. Leaving cash on a table usually causes a staff member to chase you down the street to return your "forgotten" money.
  • Watch where shoes come off: a raised entryway step (genkan) or tatami straw matting means shoes off immediately. Bathroom slippers stay strictly inside the restroom—never wear them back onto tatami!
  • Quiet shared spaces: trains, buses, elevators, and museum galleries are quiet zones. Keep phones on silent (manner mode) and hold voice calls until you're off the train.
