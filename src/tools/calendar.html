Switzerland's busiest holiday periods

  • New Year (around 29 Dec – 3 Jan): many businesses close; transport busy.
  • Golden Week (about 29 Apr – 5 May): one of the busiest domestic travel periods.
  • Obon (around mid-August): heavy travel as people return home.
  • Silver Week (some years, mid-late September).
