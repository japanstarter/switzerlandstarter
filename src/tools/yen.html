Tips for paying in Switzerland

  • When a card terminal or ATM offers to charge you in your home currency, choose yen — dynamic currency conversion usually adds a markup.
  • 7-Eleven and Switzerland Post ATMs are the most reliable for foreign cards. See our money, cash & ATMs guide and how much cash to bring.
