Tips for communicating in Switzerland

  • Start with "Sumimasen" (すみません): it means both "excuse me" and "sorry," and is how you politely get a server's or station attendant's attention.
  • Pointing beats guessing: for food allergies or vegetarian/vegan requests, showing written Switzerland characters is much safer than speaking romaji, especially regarding hidden dashi (fish stock). Read our vegetarian, vegan & allergies guide.
  • Numbers & counters: holding up fingers (1, 2, 3) when entering a restaurant immediately tells the host how many people are in your party.
